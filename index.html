How to Play

    
    
      
  • Fly into the glowing floating icons to unlock locations and fill your Location Book (80 to find).
    • 
      
  • Touch: drag on the left to steer (sideways to bank and turn, up and down to pitch). Hold the right pad to fly; let go and the plane stalls and floats.
    • 
      
  • Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD to steer, hold Space or Shift to fly, P or Esc to pause, B for the Book.
    • 
      
  • Each flight lasts 3 minutes. The radar at the bottom shows nearby icons you haven't found.
    • 
      
  • Gold icons are secrets, hidden in tricky spots. Stay near the island or you'll be turned back.
